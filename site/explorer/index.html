This site presents precomputed research outputs from the frozen COMPASS analysis. It does not execute the full COMPASS model, generate regulatory SSDs, replace chemical-specific toxicity testing, or provide real-time risk assessments.

Protection-oriented screening

Explore one frozen national sequence and its localized applications

COMPASS uses censored protective-apical evidence to estimate measured-tail probabilities and assemble compact species panels with complementary chemical coverage. The explorer presents the frozen Top-20, a complete 2,144-species continuation with cumulative expected capture for every prefix, evidence volume, and state-level localization.

The national sequence was constructed at the lower-5% target and is evaluated at broader targets without reoptimizing panel membership.

      Methods & Data

      What the explorer shows

      Measured-tail probability

      The calibrated probability that a species threshold lies within a measured lower-tail target for the stated chemical and protective effect context. Exact and bounded observations enter a censored likelihood.

      Expected capture

      The chemical-priority-weighted probability that at least one selected panel member occupies the working measured lower tail. Values are stored as probabilities and displayed as percentages.

      Complementarity

      Species are combined because their supported chemical domains can complement one another. A species' incremental gain describes its addition to the fixed sequence at the lower-5% target.

      National sequence

      One U.S.-wide sequence is selected at the lower-5% target from R1 strict focal-species leave-one-out probabilities. Ranks 1–20 reproduce the frozen R1 analysis; all 2,144 fixed-sequence prefixes are evaluated at the lower-5%, lower-10%, and lower-20% targets with the R1 target-specific working-dependence model.

      Protective evidence volume

      Evidence-volume bands count distinct valid protective analysis contexts: 1–4, 5–19, 20–49, or at least 50. The band describes how much direct evidence supports a species estimate; sensitivity is evaluated separately by the model.

      Regional localization

      State-priority chemical weights are combined with soft species relevance and method-support weights. Occurrence/NAS evidence informs relevance and follow-up needs.

      Top5-apical

      The minimum identified protective-apical threshold among available fixed Top-5 members is compared with measured full-data reference HC5 contexts to evaluate rank information.

      Chemical follow-up

      The fixed-panel measured-tail probability score identifies chemicals for additional evidence acquisition. Follow-up prioritization is interpreted within its evidence and chemical context.

      Warning endpoints

      Heartbeat, behavior, feeding, avoidance, and physiological evidence provide response-window and rank signals that guide apical confirmation.

      Data boundary

      Primary inputs include large public datasets such as EPA ECOTOX and other provider-hosted resources. Their size and provider distribution arrangements make them unsuitable for mirroring in the GitHub code repository, so users can obtain them from the official sources listed in the code package and prepare the documented local inputs. This explorer contains the compact frozen derived tables needed for its precomputed modules.
